# Hi, I'm Simon Ionashku 👋

Electrical & Electronic Engineering student at the **University of Georgia** (Certificate in Informatics, expected May 2027), building flight hardware for small satellites. My work centers on **PCB design, power electronics, RF, and embedded systems**, with a side interest in machine learning and data analysis.

## 🛰️ What I'm working on

**Small Satellite Research Laboratory (SSRL) · Hardware Engineer, PCB Design**
LEARN-Sat, a 2U CubeSat
- Designing flight PCBs for the On-Board Computer, battery/power board, electrical power system, and radio in **Altium Designer** and **KiCad**
- Taking boards from requirements and schematic capture through multilayer layout, ERC/DRC sign-off, fabrication, bring-up, and test
- Power design with hot-swap and UV/OV protection, current-limited distribution, and I²C power telemetry
- Radio front end built around an ADF5612 wideband PLL/VCO in an I/Q transceiver
- Writing Interface Control Documents (ICDs) for connectors, power rails, and I²C/SPI/UART links between boards

**NASA Ames–Sponsored Senior Capstone · Hardware Lead**
- Leading hardware design of a CubeSat electrical power system (EPS) for NASA Ames' hardware-in-the-loop flight-software testbed
- Covers solar MPPT charging, battery management, and DC-DC power distribution
